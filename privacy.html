Who runs this site

This site is run by Victor Tarra, an Information Systems student at De La Salle–College of Saint Benilde, as a personal portfolio. For any privacy question, email tarravdc@proton.me.

What I collect

I don't collect personal information from you just for visiting. The site has no sign-up, no analytics scripts, no advertising and no tracking cookies, and I don't see who visits.

I only receive personal information if you choose to send it to me, by email or through the contact form. That is covered under When you contact me.

What's stored on your device

If you use the light/dark theme switch, your browser saves your choice in its local storage under the name theme, with the value light or dark. It stays on your device, is never sent to me, and is used only to show the theme you picked next time. You can remove it by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. According to GitHub's documentation, when someone visits a GitHub Pages site, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not they're signed in to GitHub. I don't have access to these logs. GitHub's handling of this data is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Content from other services

Some parts of the page are loaded from other companies' servers. When your browser requests them, those servers receive the technical information every web request includes, such as your IP address and browser type. Each service handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Google Fonts
Loads the Overpass typeface on every page. Google Privacy Policy
ghchart.rshah.org
Draws the GitHub contribution chart in the Credentials section. Run by an independent developer, not by GitHub.
GitHub
Supplies the CSB-DSTALGO organization picture. GitHub General Privacy Statement
Jotform
Provides the contact form. It loads only if you open "Send a message from this page" or follow the contact form link. Jotform Privacy Policy

When you contact me

If you email me or use the contact form, I receive what you choose to include, usually your name, email address and message.

  • I use it only to read and reply to your message.
  • I don't sell it, rent it out, or add you to a mailing list.
  • I keep it only as long as the conversation needs, and I'll delete it if you ask.

Email to my address is handled by Proton Mail, and form entries are processed and stored by Jotform before they reach me. Their privacy policies apply to that handling.

Links to other sites

The portfolio links to other websites, including LinkedIn, GitHub, Facebook, certificate verification pages and my project sites. Once you leave this site, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Your rights

You can ask me to tell you what personal information I hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email tarravdc@proton.me and I'll respond as soon as I reasonably can.

If you're in the Philippines, you also have rights as a data subject under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). These include the right to complain to the National Privacy Commission.

This site is a general-audience portfolio and isn't directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the site starts using new services, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
